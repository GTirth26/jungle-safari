Blackbuck Safari

Species: Blackbuck (Antilope cervicapra)

Habitat: Grasslands & scrublands

Typical Duration: 1 - 2 hours

Best time: October – March

Popular Parks: Velavadar, Rehekuri, Ranibennur

Official Booking / Info

About the Blackbuck

The blackbuck is a native Indian antelope known for its striking appearance and speed. Males have dark brown to black coats with long, spiraled horns; females and juveniles are lighter in color. They are grazers that prefer open grasslands where they form herds — sometimes numbering in the hundreds.

Where to see Blackbuck (selected locations)

  • Velavadar Blackbuck National Park, Gujarat — largest and most reliable place to see large herds; good birdlife too.
  • Rehekuri Blackbuck Sanctuary, Maharashtra — compact sanctuary, excellent for short safaris and photography.
  • Ranibennur Blackbuck Sanctuary, Karnataka — mixed landscape and stable populations.
  • Point Calimere, Tamil Nadu — coastal reserve with occasional blackbuck sightings along with birds.

Safari Experience & Practical Info

Most blackbuck safaris are sunrise or late afternoon drives. Expect:

  • Jeep/jeep-like vehicle or park transport.
  • Guided interpretive talk by a forest guide.
  • Wildlife viewing, photography stops (subject to park rules).

What to bring: binoculars, camera with zoom lens, hat, water, sunscreen, light jacket (mornings can be chilly).

Rules & Safety

  • Always follow the guide's instructions.
  • Stay inside designated vehicles or areas unless supervised.
  • Do not feed or attempt to touch the animals.
  • Keep noise to a minimum and avoid flash photography if requested.
  • Carry and dispose of waste responsibly.

Booking & Contacts

For government-run sanctuaries, booking is usually available through the state forest department website (search "Velavadar booking" or "Rehekuri booking"). Private grassland reserves will have direct booking pages or contact emails. If you want, I can add direct booking links for specific parks (Velavadar, Rehekuri).

Suggested Add-ons

  • Grassland nature walk (where permitted)
  • Birdwatching tour — grasslands support many resident and migratory birds
  • Visit nearby cultural or heritage sites (depends on park)
