About The Resort

Sanctuary in the Heart of Gir

The Premier Gir Resort is your gateway to the world's only home of the Asiatic Lion. Nestled on the fringe of the Gir National Park, we blend rustic jungle charm with modern luxury.

Whether you are looking for an adventurous jungle safari, a relaxing spa weekend, or a family getaway, our resort provides the perfect ecosystem.

  • Private Jungle Cottages
  • Expert Naturalist Guides
  • Organic Farm-to-Table
  • Cultural Evening Shows

Location: Located just 2km from the Sinh Sadan entry gate.
Climate: Enjoy pleasant mornings and cool evenings.
Policies: Plastic-free zone. Quiet hours after 10 PM to respect wildlife.
