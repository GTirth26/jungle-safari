Ranthambore National Park - Jungle Safari

Location: Rajasthan, India

Nearest City: Sawai Madhopur

Coordinates: 26.0173° N, 76.5026° E

Area: 1,334 km²

Established: 1980

Governing Body: Forest Department, Rajasthan

More Informatin

About Ranthambore National Park

Ranthambore National Park is one of the largest and most renowned national parks in northern India. Located in the Sawai Madhopur district of Rajasthan, it is famous for its population of Bengal tigers and its picturesque landscape, which includes lakes, ancient ruins, and dense forests.

Wildlife

While famous for tigers, the park is a rich habitat for many other species:

  • Bengal Tiger
  • Leopard
  • Sloth Bear
  • Sambar Deer
  • Spotted Deer (Chital)
  • Indian Wild Boar
  • Crocodile
  • Over 270 species of birds

Flora

The vegetation is mainly dry deciduous forest type. The dominant tree is 'Dhok', but the park also features:

  • Banyan
  • Peepal
  • Jamun
  • Tendu
  • Ber

Safari Experience

Safaris in Ranthambore are conducted in open-top jeeps (6-seaters) and canters (20-seaters). The park is divided into 10 safari zones, and vehicles are assigned a zone randomly. Morning and evening safaris are available, and advance booking is highly recommended.

Attractions

  • Ranthambore Fort (UNESCO World Heritage Site)
  • Trinetra Ganesh Temple
  • Padam Talao Lake
  • Raj Bagh Ruins
  • Jogi Mahal

Visitor Information

Best time to visit: October to April (Park is closed during monsoon from July to September)

Entry permits: Must be booked online in advance

Accommodations: A wide range of hotels and resorts are available in Sawai Madhopur
