About Jungle Retreat Resort

Jungle Retreat Resort is a peaceful nature stay located near Vansda National Park. It’s surrounded by lush greenery, making it ideal for guests who want a calm and eco-friendly holiday. The resort focuses on sustainability, local culture, and close-to-nature experiences.

Accommodation

The resort offers a mix of rustic cottages and modern rooms designed to blend with the forest surroundings.

  • Deluxe Forest Cottages with private sit-out
  • Luxury Tents for an authentic jungle feel
  • Family Rooms for groups and large families

Facilities & Amenities

  • Multi-cuisine restaurant serving local & Indian food
  • Outdoor campfire and barbeque area
  • Swimming pool with forest view
  • Nature walks and birdwatching tours
  • Free Wi-Fi and parking

Activities & Experiences

  • Guided forest trails and short hikes
  • Evening campfire with local tribal dance (on request)
  • Visit to Vansda National Park and Gira Waterfalls
  • Photography and birdwatching sessions

How to Reach

Nearest Town: Waghai (8 km)

By Road: Accessible from Navsari (65 km) and Surat (120 km)

By Train: Waghai railway station is the nearest stop

Visitor Guidelines

  • Check-in with valid ID proof.
  • Avoid loud music and respect nature.
  • Follow safety instructions during forest walks.
  • Do not litter or harm plants and wildlife.
